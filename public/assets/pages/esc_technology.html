The Technology Layer supports the Data and Application Layers. This includes the infrastructure, platforms, and tools used to support those layers. It encompasses the hardware, software, and network components that enable the data and application(s) to function effectively and securely.

Architects working at this layer focus on ensuring scalability, reliability, security, and performance of the technology stack. They make decisions regarding premise and cloud services, databases, middleware, and other technical resources that form the backbone of the system.

Key architectural considerations at the Technology Layer include selecting appropriate technologies, designing for fault tolerance, optimizing resource utilization, and ensuring compliance with industry standards and best practices. But this is only one part of the overall architecture. The Enterprise Architecture practice guides the alignment of technology with business capability, ensuring that the technology layer supports the broader objectives of the organization.
